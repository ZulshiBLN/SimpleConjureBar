# Store gallery

The images in `.previews/`, in this order: for each, the title `tools/preview-title.py` puts on
it, and the caption it gets in the CurseForge and Wago galleries (CurseForge allows at most 256
characters). Upload the images and paste the captions by hand on both sites with each release
whose previews change. This is the 1.1.1 set of ruleset plan 2026-10-25-store-screenshots; images
01 and 04 follow later.

## 02-intellect-and-tracking.jpg

**Title:** Arcane Intellect, your way

**Caption:** The Intellect button glows and counts who still needs the buff; its tooltip shows every click. On the Tracking tab you set how early to renew and from how many in need it casts Arcane Brilliance.

## 03-conjure-and-trade.jpg

**Title:** Water and food for everyone

**Caption:** Left-click to use, right-click to conjure, middle-click to trade: the trade window opens with water and food for your target's level. A player who uses no mana gets food only. You still confirm the trade yourself.

## 05-teleports-and-armor.jpg

**Title:** Teleports, portals and armor

**Caption:** One button each: a right-click opens the list, your choice is remembered. Rune counts on Teleport and Portal warn you when you run low. Shown in demo mode, which shows every button before you learn it.

## 06-macros.jpg

**Title:** Macros in one click

**Caption:** Polymorph with a raid marker, Ice Block that ends on a second press, and stopcasting macros for Fire Blast, Frost Nova, Blink or Counterspell, created from one tab.

## 07-general-and-languages.jpg

**Title:** Your look, eleven languages

**Caption:** Horizontal or vertical, scale, border, colors and window transparency; eleven languages, switched in game without a reload. The minimap button and the AddOn menu open these settings too.

## 08-bug-report.jpg

**Title:** Report a bug in one click

**Caption:** /scb bug writes the report for you and docks it beside the settings. It filters your character, realm, guild and current group, shows everything it holds, and gives you the code and the link to the form.
